The Stark Reality

The Paradox of Modern Food Systems

Globally, more than a third of all food produced never reaches a human stomach. Meanwhile, hunger remains one of humanity's most urgent crises. It isn't a scarcity problem — it's a logistics failure.

Over 33% of the world's food supply ends up rotting in landfills, producing methane that accounts for 8% to 10% of total global greenhouse gas emissions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about food safety, legal protection, logistics, and getting started.

Yes, 100%. Under the Bill Emerson Good Samaritan Food Donation Act (and corresponding national food donation protection laws globally), donors who donate apparently wholesome food in good faith to non-profit organizations are completely shielded from civil and criminal liability. FoodBridge additionally signs a formal indemnification agreement with all participating commercial donors.

We accept:

  • Freshly cooked meals: Buffet surplus, restaurant batches, catered items maintained at safe holding temperatures.
  • Uncooked raw produce: Surplus vegetables, fruits, dairy, and grains from wholesalers and supermarkets.
  • Baked goods: Bread, baguettes, buns, and sealed pastries from artisan bakeries.
  • Packaged shelf-stable goods: Canned goods, dry pasta, juice, and pantry items before best-by date.

We do not accept leftover plate scrapings, unsealed raw shellfish, or food that has been exposed without temperature control for more than 2 hours.

Every rescue courier carries calibrated digital infrared thermometers and food-grade insulated bags. Hot food must be ≥ 60°C (140°F) at pickup, or chilled immediately to ≤ 4°C (40°F). The courier captures digital photo proof and logs temperature readings onto our secure chain-of-custody app before sealing containers with tamper-evident tape.

No. Basic food donation is completely free for food donors. In fact, participating donors save thousands of dollars every quarter on commercial waste disposal tipping fees. Additionally, donors receive automated tax-deductible charitable receipts (80G / 501(c)(3)) and audited ESG carbon reduction reports.

Our average door-to-pickup time is 38 minutes. For planned events (weddings, banquets, conventions), donors can also schedule pickups hours or days in advance with guaranteed courier dispatch.

NGOs, orphanages, day centers, and soup kitchens submit their registered non-profit license, health & hygiene clearance, and storage facility photos. Our community team reviews and approves applications within 24 hours so that food reaches legitimate, deserving beneficiaries immediately.
